Talking Tuner

Play a string and the phone says which string it is and whether it is low or high, for example “low E string, low”. When the string comes in tune, the phone dings and says so. Everything runs on the phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Talking Tuner, build 15.

Guitar strings, low to high: E2, A2, D3, G3, B3, E4.

Guitar selected. Press Start listening.

Keep this page open and the phone awake while tuning. iOS pauses page audio if the page loses focus or the phone locks. The microphone only works on a secure page, so open this over HTTPS.
